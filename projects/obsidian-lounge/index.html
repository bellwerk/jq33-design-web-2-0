Obsidian Lounge

Self-initiated concept study; illustrative visual; not completed client work.

Illustrative lounge concept board placeholder for a self-initiated study; not a photograph of completed client work.

Study focus
An intimate hospitality lounge testing low-light orientation, varied seating clusters, and a central service element.
Reference market
Hypothetical evening hospitality market
Study status
Self-initiated concept study
Visual provenance
Locally created illustrative placeholder

Design questions

  • How can guests read circulation clearly in a deliberately low-light setting?
  • Which seating geometries support both privacy and social energy?
  • Can reflected cobalt light establish orientation without becoming decorative glare?

Proposed direction

Anchor the plan with a charcoal oval bar, arrange soft-white seating islands around it, and use restrained cobalt light lines to mark movement.

Program assumptions

  • A central service bar visible from the entry
  • A mix of paired, group, and perimeter seating
  • Clear service paths that do not divide the guest zones
