Self-initiated spatial research

Concept studies

Five speculative commercial interior studies used to investigate circulation, program, atmosphere, and material direction. None is presented as commissioned or completed work.

Concept study · illustrative visual · not completed client work.

Office · Self-initiated concept study Bruton Place IV A compact reception and work floor organized around a clear arrival axis, focused work settings, and shared project space. Self-initiated concept study; illustrative visual; not completed client work.
Retail · Self-initiated concept study Ethereal Gallery A quiet retail gallery that tests flexible display planes, measured pauses, and a legible path through a changing product edit. Self-initiated concept study; illustrative visual; not completed client work.
Lounge · Self-initiated concept study Obsidian Lounge An intimate hospitality lounge testing low-light orientation, varied seating clusters, and a central service element. Self-initiated concept study; illustrative visual; not completed client work.
Showroom · Self-initiated concept study Vortex Showroom A product showroom organized as a paced demonstration loop with a strong arrival marker and adaptable perimeter bays. Self-initiated concept study; illustrative visual; not completed client work.
Salon · Self-initiated concept study Canvas Studios A salon program that studies station rhythm, shared preparation zones, and a calm progression from arrival to service. Self-initiated concept study; illustrative visual; not completed client work.
