Self-initiated Office concept study

Bruton Place IV

Self-initiated concept study; illustrative visual; not completed client work.

Illustrative office concept board placeholder for a self-initiated study; not a photograph of completed client work.

Study focus
A compact reception and work floor organized around a clear arrival axis, focused work settings, and shared project space.
Reference market
Hypothetical urban office market
Study status
Self-initiated concept study
Visual provenance
Locally created illustrative placeholder

Design questions

  • How can the entry provide orientation without a conventional reception desk?
  • Where should acoustic transitions occur between shared and focused work?
  • Can one continuous cobalt element connect arrival, storage, and wayfinding?

Proposed direction

Use a long cobalt datum to guide arrival, then offset charcoal work zones with soft-white circulation bands and acoustic screens.

Program assumptions

  • A reception point with an adjacent waiting zone
  • A mix of focused desks, an enclosed meeting room, and an open project table
  • A material palette that separates public and focused-work zones
