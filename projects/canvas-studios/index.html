Illustrative salon concept board placeholder for a self-initiated study; not a photograph of completed client work.

Study focus
A salon program that studies station rhythm, shared preparation zones, and a calm progression from arrival to service.
Reference market
Hypothetical independent salon market
Study status
Self-initiated concept study
Visual provenance
Locally created illustrative placeholder

Design questions

  • What station rhythm supports clear movement without feeling repetitive?
  • How can shared storage remain accessible while keeping the floor visually calm?
  • Can lighting distinguish consultation, styling, and wash activities?

Proposed direction

Build a measured sequence of cobalt-framed mirrors along a charcoal service spine, with soft-white circulation and a screened wash zone.

Program assumptions

  • A reception point with a compact waiting area
  • A repeated line of styling stations with shared preparation storage
  • A screened wash zone separated from the arrival view
