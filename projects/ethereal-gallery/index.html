Illustrative retail concept board placeholder for a self-initiated study; not a photograph of completed client work.

Study focus
A quiet retail gallery that tests flexible display planes, measured pauses, and a legible path through a changing product edit.
Reference market
Hypothetical independent retail market
Study status
Self-initiated concept study
Visual provenance
Locally created illustrative placeholder

Design questions

  • How little visual infrastructure is needed to make each display feel deliberate?
  • Can circulation alternate between compression and release without becoming confusing?
  • How might light define zones when the display arrangement changes?

Proposed direction

Compose a soft-white field of slim plinths and translucent screens, punctuated by cobalt thresholds and a charcoal consultation niche.

Program assumptions

  • A small welcome point and a sequence of flexible display zones
  • Movable plinths for changing product groupings
  • A consultation niche screened from the primary circulation path
